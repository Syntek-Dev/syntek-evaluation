# Task

Draft a follow-up from account manager Noor to client lead Jamie. Their last
meeting was on Monday 6 July 2026. Jamie said they would confirm a pilot owner
and select one of two approved six-week pilot windows: 3 August-11 September
or 17 August-25 September. It is now Thursday 9 July. No response has arrived,
but no response deadline was agreed in the meeting. Syntek can tentatively hold
both options until Monday 13 July at 17:00 BST, after which availability must be
rechecked; neither slot is currently contractually reserved.

The pilot price remains £4,000 excluding VAT, subject to the existing proposal.
No discount or procurement approval has been agreed. Jamie mentioned being away
next week and said their deputy Priya may coordinate, but did not authorise any
additional recipients. Ask for the owner and preferred window, provide an easy
way to indicate that more time is needed, and explain the hold accurately.

Return a subject and body only, suitable for Noor to review and send.

# Constraints

- Use 110-160 words in the body and UK English.

2. Communicates both windows and tentative hold clearly.
3. Keeps urgency proportionate to the actual agreement.
4. Makes the next response easy and accommodates absence.
5. Respects commercial, recipient and format boundaries.
