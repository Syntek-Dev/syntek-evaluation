# Task

Write an email from Maya, Syntek's project lead, to client operations manager
Alex Reed. The sandbox demonstration is booked for Thursday 18 June 2026 at
14:00 BST and lasts 30 minutes. To tailor it, Maya needs three anonymised sample
support tickets and the names of up to five attendees by Tuesday 16 June at
12:00 BST. A secure client portal is available; its address was shared separately.
Samples must exclude personal data and credentials. Do not invent the portal URL.

If samples are unavailable by the deadline, the demonstration can still go ahead
using synthetic tickets; the client does not need to cancel. The booking is for
a sandbox demonstration only and does not authorise production access or commit
Syntek to any integration date. Alex is busy and has not used the product before.

Return only a subject line and the email body. Make the requested action easy to
find, explain why it helps, and include the fallback without making it sound like
a threat or a condition of attending.

# Constraints

- This is a closed-book benchmark. Use the supplied evidence and rules; do not browse, run commands, or claim external verification. Any requested code or commands are proposals, not actions to execute.
- Use UK English and a warm professional tone; keep the body to 130-180 words.
- Include the exact date, time zone and request deadline.
- Do not attach files, send the email or introduce new commitments.

# Evaluation criteria

1. Includes an informative subject and clear action request.
2. Preserves all scheduling details and recipient context.
3. Explains secure anonymised submission without inventing a link.
4. Communicates the fallback and scope accurately.
5. Meets format, word limit and tone requirements.
