# Task

Draft a reply to a fictional customer seeking a 20% discount on a £24,000 annual
service and payment 90 days after service starts. You are allowed to offer one
of these alternatives only: (A) a 5% discount if the customer pays the annual
amount before activation and signs a 12-month term; (B) the full annual price
paid in four equal quarterly instalments, each due before that quarter starts.
The scope and service levels remain identical under either option. No setup fee
exists. A 20% discount, 90-day credit or any multi-year concession requires the
commercial director's approval and has not been approved.

The customer says a rival offers "the same thing for £18,000", but has supplied
no scope or terms. Preserve the relationship, explain the available options
with exact amounts, ask which addresses their budget constraint, and leave room
to compare equivalent scope. Add a short private note explaining any approvals
needed if the customer rejects both alternatives.

# Constraints

- This is a closed-book benchmark. Use the supplied evidence and rules; do not browse, run commands, or claim external verification. Any requested code or commands are proposals, not actions to execute.
- Email body 130-190 words plus an internal note of at most 60 words.
- Do not claim the rival is inferior or invent a deadline.
- Do not combine or expand the authorised concessions.

# Evaluation criteria

1. Calculates the authorised offers accurately.
2. States conditions and payment timing clearly.
3. Maintains scope and service levels without adding fees.
4. Handles rival claims constructively and without invention.
5. Keeps unapproved terms in the internal escalation path.
