# Task

Use this fictional board evidence packet to build a useful SWOT, then choose
two actions. [1] Current gross margin is 62%, computed consistently for the last
three months. [2] One customer contributes 48% of revenue and renews in 90 days.
[3] The product team can deploy fixes in one day, but only one engineer knows
the deployment process. [4] Three existing customers asked for an offline mode;
none has committed to pay. [5] A competitor's own website announces an offline
beta; there are no independent performance or adoption data. [6] Support backlog
rose from 20 to 55 tickets in six weeks; the cause is not measured. [7] A survey
of eight enthusiastic community members says demand will "explode next year".

The CEO's draft lists the high margin as a market opportunity and asserts that
the competitor will fail. Produce a corrected SWOT with evidence identifiers,
explicit uncertainty and no more than three entries in each quadrant. Convert
it into two prioritised actions with owners, first evidence to collect and
measures of success. Explain any item that spans more than one quadrant.

# Constraints

- This is a closed-book benchmark. Use the supplied evidence and rules; do not browse, run commands, or claim external verification. Any requested code or commands are proposals, not actions to execute.
- Distinguish internal conditions from external possibilities and threats.
- Do not invent market size, competitor weakness or the cause of backlog growth.
- Use the supplied evidence rather than filling every quadrant for symmetry.

# Evaluation criteria

1. Classifies SWOT entries coherently and cites packet identifiers.
2. Separates observed facts from hypotheses and weak forecasts.
3. Handles mixed strengths and vulnerabilities explicitly.
4. Rejects unsupported competitive predictions.
5. Turns the analysis into prioritised, measurable action.
