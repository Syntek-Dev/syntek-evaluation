# Task

Create a stakeholder and decision plan for a fictional customer-support AI pilot.
Sponsor Nia controls the £30,000 budget and pilot scope. Security lead Dev alone
can approve production access. Operations lead Jo owns the support rota and must
accept handover. Six support agents will use the pilot daily but have no budget
authority. Client contact Lee coordinates sample documents but cannot authorise
data processing. The client's information owner, who can authorise those samples,
has not yet been identified. Supplier representative Pat can advise on product
configuration but is not permitted to view customer documents.

Nia asks Pat to attend every meeting "for transparency" and wants to begin using
real customer documents tomorrow. Support agents worry the pilot will be used
to rank their individual performance; no such purpose has been approved. A
decision on a synthetic-data pilot is possible without production access.

Provide a concise responsibility/decision matrix, tailored engagement cadence,
an escalation path and the next three actions. Explain how to include affected
staff and make progress while respecting the stated authority and information
boundaries. Do not invent consent or approval.

# Constraints

- This is a closed-book benchmark. Use the supplied evidence and rules; do not browse, run commands, or claim external verification. Any requested code or commands are proposals, not actions to execute.
- Assign accountability according to the facts rather than seniority alone.
- Limit meeting information and attendance to the relevant purpose.
- Do not promise employment outcomes or authorise a new staff-monitoring purpose.

# Evaluation criteria

1. Maps decisions to actual authority and identifies missing ownership.
2. Includes affected users with meaningful feedback routes.
3. Protects documents and sensitive discussions from unauthorised participants.
4. Sets proportionate engagement and escalation mechanisms.
5. Proposes immediate progress without assumed approvals.
